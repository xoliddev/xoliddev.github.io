Oila Hamyon

A family accountant inside Telegram. Write “nonga 15 ming” the way you'd say it, and the bot records the expense with its category and wallet, keeps every wallet balanced to the tiyin and answers questions about the month.

Telegram ichidagi oilaviy hisobchi. “nonga 15 ming” deb odatdagidek yozing: bot xarajatni kategoriya va hamyoni bilan yozadi, har bir hamyonni tiyinigacha aniq yuritadi va oy haqidagi savollarga javob beradi.

Interactive browser demo. It uses the bot's real texts, keyboards and reply formats and runs entirely in your browser. The AI parsing happens on the server in production, so a small rule-based parser stands in for it here.

Brauzerdagi interaktiv demo. Botning haqiqiy matnlari, tugmalari va javob ko'rinishlari ishlatilgan, hammasi brauzeringizda ishlaydi. Productionda matnni server tomonida AI tushunadi, bu yerda uning o'rnida kichik qoidaviy parser ishlaydi.

Why there is no Telegram linkNega Telegram havolasi yo'q

The bot is private by design: the first person to press /start creates the only family, and everyone else needs that family's invite link. A stranger gets “this is one family's private bot”, so the demo lives here instead.Bot ataylab yopiq: /start ni birinchi bosgan odam yagona oilani yaratadi, qolganlar shu oilaning taklif havolasi bilan qo'shiladi.
