Smena

A back-office bot for a restaurant team. Staff apply and check in from Telegram, the GPS check-in only works inside the restaurant's radius, managers hand out advances with a receipt to every admin, and the month closes with an Excel payroll.

Restoran jamoasi uchun ichki bot. Xodimlar Telegram orqali ariza beradi va ishga keladi, GPS orqali kelish faqat restoran radiusi ichida ishlaydi, rahbar avans beradi va har bir admin xabar oladi, oy Excel hisobot bilan yopiladi.

Interactive browser demo. Two Telegram chats, the employee's and the manager's, run side by side in your browser with the bot's real texts and buttons. Locations are fake and chosen on the map. The sample business is “Demo Cafe”.

Brauzerdagi interaktiv demo. Ikki Telegram chati, xodimniki va rahbarniki, botning haqiqiy matn va tugmalari bilan brauzeringizda yonma-yon ishlaydi. Joylashuv soxta va xaritada tanlanadi. Namunaviy korxona “Demo Cafe”.
