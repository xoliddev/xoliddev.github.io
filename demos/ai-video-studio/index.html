AI Video Studio

Videos in Uzbek made entirely in code: a script becomes Gemini TTS voices, a timeline, frames rendered by headless Chrome and a final ffmpeg mix. None of them were cut in a video editor.

Final renders. These are the finished videos, re-encoded to 720p H.264 for the web. Tap a poster to load and play it; nothing downloads until you do.
