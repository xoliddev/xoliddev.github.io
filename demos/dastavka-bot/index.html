Dastavka

Ordering and delivery for a single café, entirely inside Telegram. The customer orders from a menu, the admin accepts it and dispatches a courier, and the courier closes the delivery only after the customer confirms they received it.

Bitta kafe uchun buyurtma va yetkazib berish, to'liq Telegram ichida. Mijoz menyudan buyurtma beradi, admin qabul qilib kuryer tayinlaydi, kuryer esa mijoz qabul qilganini tasdiqlagandan keyingina yetkazishni yopadi.

Interactive browser demo. Three Telegram chats, customer, admin and courier, share one order in your browser. Every message is the bot's real Uzbek text from its lexicon. The café is the sample “Demo Cafe”.

Brauzerdagi interaktiv demo. Uchta Telegram chati, mijoz, admin va kuryer, brauzeringizda bitta buyurtmani bo'lishadi. Har bir xabar botning lug'atidagi haqiqiy matn. Kafe namunaviy “Demo Cafe”.
