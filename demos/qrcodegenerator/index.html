QR Code Generator

Send the bot a voice note, a photo or a few lines of text and it builds a small landing page for them, then hands you a QR code. Whoever scans it sees the page and hears the voice. Used for gift cards and printed invitations.

Botga ovozli xabar, surat yoki matn yuboring: u ular uchun kichik sahifa yaratib, QR kod beradi. Skanerlagan odam sahifani ko'radi va ovozni eshitadi. Sovg'a kartalari va bosma taklifnomalar uchun.

Interactive browser demo. The chat uses the bot's real texts and buttons, the landing page is the bot's real template, and the QR code is generated in your browser and really scans.

Brauzerdagi interaktiv demo. Chatda botning haqiqiy matn va tugmalari, sahifa botning haqiqiy shabloni, QR kod esa brauzeringizda yaratiladi va haqiqatan skanerlanadi.
