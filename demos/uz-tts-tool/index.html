uz-tts-tool

A small Python tool that turns Uzbek text of any length, from a post to a whole book, into one natural-sounding MP3 with Gemini's TTS models. These are its real outputs, untouched.

Istalgan uzunlikdagi o'zbekcha matnni, postdan to butun kitobgacha, Gemini TTS modellari bilan bitta tabiiy ovozli MP3 ga aylantiradigan kichik Python vositasi. Bular uning haqiqiy, o'zgartirilmagan natijalari.

Real output. Every clip on this page is an MP3 the tool generated, copied from the repository's samples/ folder. The waveforms are drawn in your browser from the audio itself.

Haqiqiy natija. Bu sahifadagi har bir klip vosita yaratgan MP3, repozitoriyning samples/ papkasidan olingan. To'lqin shakllari brauzeringizda audioning o'zidan chiziladi.

What the tool doesVosita nima qiladi

  • Splits text of any length on sentence boundaries into ~650-character chunks, never across a paragraph.Istalgan uzunlikdagi matnni gap chegarasida ~650 belgilik bo'laklarga bo'ladi, xatboshidan o'tkazmaydi.
  • Normalises every Uzbek apostrophe variant so o' and g' are pronounced the same way.O'zbekcha apostrofning barcha turlarini bir xil qiladi, o' va g' bir xil talaffuz qilinadi.
  • Caches finished chunks, so an interrupted multi-hour book resumes where it stopped; rotates API keys when one hits its daily quota.Tayyor bo'laklarni keshlaydi: soatlab davom etgan kitob to'xtasa, qolgan joyidan davom etadi; kunlik limit tugasa keyingi API kalitiga o'tadi.
  • Trims Gemini's end-of-clip noise burst, joins the clips with natural pauses and encodes one MP3 at −16 LUFS with a timestamp index.Gemini klip oxiridagi shovqinni kesadi, bo'laklarni tabiiy pauzalar bilan ulaydi va −16 LUFS'dagi bitta MP3 hamda vaqt belgilari faylini yaratadi.
