Word Chain bot

An English word-chain game for Telegram. Each word starts with the last letter of the previous one, and the bot opponent only knows the words of its CEFR level. Play a full game against it here.

Telegram uchun inglizcha so'z zanjiri o'yini. Har bir so'z oldingi so'zning oxirgi harfi bilan boshlanadi, bot-raqib esa faqat o'z CEFR darajasidagi so'zlarni biladi. Shu yerda unga qarshi to'liq o'yin o'ynang.

Interactive browser demo. The game engine, bot opponent and word list were ported from the bot's Python code to JavaScript and run entirely in your browser. Every message and button is the bot's real Uzbek text. Nothing is sent to Telegram.

Brauzerdagi interaktiv demo. O'yin qoidalari, bot-raqib va so'zlar ro'yxati botning Python kodidan JavaScript'ga ko'chirilgan va to'liq brauzeringizda ishlaydi. Har bir xabar va tugma botning haqiqiy matni. Telegram'ga hech narsa yuborilmaydi.

How to play

Qanday o'ynaladi

  1. Press 🤖 Bot bilan o'ynash, then pick the bot's level. Change your own level with 📶 Daraja.
  2. 🤖 Bot bilan o'ynash tugmasini bosing va bot darajasini tanlang. O'z darajangizni 📶 Daraja orqali o'zgartirasiz.
  3. Type any English word. The bot answers with a word that starts with your last letter.
  4. Istalgan inglizcha so'zni yozing. Bot oxirgi harfingiz bilan boshlanadigan so'z bilan javob beradi.
  5. Three wrong words, a timeout or 🏳️ Taslim bo'lish loses the game. 💡 Yordam gives a masked hint twice per game.
  6. Uchta noto'g'ri so'z, vaqt tugashi yoki 🏳️ Taslim bo'lish mag'lubiyat. 💡 Yordam har o'yinda ikki marta yashirin maslahat beradi.
