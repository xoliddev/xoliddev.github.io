Benzinchi Bola

Loyalty coins, fuel delivery and targeted marketing for a chain of fuel stations.

Yoqilg'i shoxobchalari tarmog'i uchun bonus coinlar, yoqilg'ini yetkazib berish va maqsadli marketing.

API, admin panel, customer app, courier appAPI, admin panel, mijoz ilovasi, kuryer ilovasi

  • Coin ledger. A PostgreSQL double-entry ledger in the NestJS API: every bonus earned, spent or refunded is a balanced pair of entries, so balances can always be rebuilt and audited.
  • Cashier screen. The React admin finds a customer by QR scan, plate number, phone or a 6-digit code, and gives or spends coins in one step. Each staff role sees only its own sections.
  • Customer app. Expo app with SMS login, a live balance and loyalty tiers, a QR code that changes every 60 seconds, and fuel delivery with cash, card, coins or a mix. It opens from an offline cache when the network is slow.
  • Courier app and live tracking. Background GPS from the courier's phone, pushed over Socket.IO to the customer's map with a full status chain.
  • Payments and marketing. Payme and Click checkout; banners and push campaigns targeted by car brand, fuel type, balance and oil-change date.
  • Coin hisobi. NestJS API ichida PostgreSQL ikki tomonlama yozuvli hisob: har bir olingan, sarflangan yoki qaytarilgan bonus muvozanatli juft yozuv, shuning uchun qoldiqni har doim qayta tiklash va tekshirish mumkin.
  • Kassa ekrani. React admin xaridorni QR, avtoraqam, telefon yoki 6 xonali kod bo'yicha topadi va bonusni bir amalda beradi yoki yechadi. Har bir rol faqat o'z bo'limlarini ko'radi.
  • Mijoz ilovasi. Expo ilova: SMS orqali kirish, jonli balans va sodiqlik darajalari, har 60 soniyada yangilanadigan QR kod, naqd, karta, bonus yoki aralash to'lov bilan yoqilg'i yetkazish. Internet sekin bo'lsa oflayn keshdan ochiladi.
  • Kuryer ilovasi va jonli kuzatuv. Kuryer telefonidan fondagi GPS Socket.IO orqali xaridor xaritasiga uzatiladi, holatlar zanjiri bilan.
  • To'lov va marketing. Payme va Click; mashina markasi, yoqilg'i turi, balans va moy almashtirish sanasi bo'yicha bannerlar va push kampaniyalar.

NestJS 11, Fastify, PostgreSQL, Drizzle ORM, Socket.IO, React 19, Vite, Leaflet, Expo SDK 58, Payme, Click

  • API. PostgreSQL ustida Kysely bilan Fastify 5, boshidan oxirigacha tiplangan, React admin va Expo ilova bilan umumiy.

Fastify 5, Kysely, PostgreSQL 18, React, Expo, Docker, Caddy
